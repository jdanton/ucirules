MEMORANDUM

28.09.2026

PART 1 GENERAL ORGANISATION OF CYCLING AS A SPORT Rules amendments applying on 01.01.2027

Chapter I LICENCE-HOLDERS

§ 1 Licences

  • 1.1.006 ter For Road discipline, each National Federation must set out in its regulations the provisions applicable to a rider's change of club during the season. These provisions must cover at least the following points:

  • the maximum number of club changes permitted within a single season;

  • the procedure to be followed to carry out the change, including the applicable deadlines and, where relevant, the periods during which a change is permitted;

  • the supporting documents and evidence required to validate the change;

  • the specific conditions applicable where the change involves at least one club affiliated to another National Federation;

  • the prior agreement of both the club being left and the new club;

  • the effective updating, following the change, of the name of the club shown on the rider's licence.

(text introduced on 01.01.27)

§ 2 Categories of riders

Cycling for all
  • 1.1.039 A cycling for all licence is issued to the cyclists practising cycling as a leisure activity. This licence shall give access only to events on the cycling for all and gravel calendars.

(text modified on 01.01.05; 01.10.26)
