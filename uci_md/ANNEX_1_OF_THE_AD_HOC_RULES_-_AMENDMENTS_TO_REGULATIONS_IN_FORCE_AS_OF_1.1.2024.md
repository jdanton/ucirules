**UCI CYCLING REGULATIONS** 

## **Annex 1 of the Ad Hoc Rules** 

## **Conditions of Participation for individual neutral riders of Russian and Belarusian sporting nationality and their support personnel.** 

## **Preamble** 

Pursuant to the recommendations issued by the International Olympic Committee (IOC) on 28 March 2023 and the Ad Hoc Rules adopted by the UCI Management Committee in response to Russia’s invasion of Ukraine, the UCI enacts the following conditions for the participation of riders of Russian and Belarusian sporting nationality and their support personnel in events on the UCI International Calendar: 

|**Participation**|Except for the participation of riders with their team in accordance<br>with article 5 b. of the Ad Hoc Rules, riders of Russian and<br>Belarusian sporting nationality and their support personnel may<br>only participate in events on the UCI International Calendar in an<br>individual and neutral capacity, and in no case as representatives<br>of the Russian Federation or the Republic of Belarus, their<br>National Federations or NOCs.<br>A rider participating in events on the UCI International Calendar<br>under the previous paragraph is considered as an "_Individual_<br>_Neutral Athlete_".<br>Individual Neutral Athletes may not participate in events which<br>are based on a classification by teams. Their participation is only<br>authorised in events based on individual classification.<br>With regard to the support personnel, only persons holding a<br>high-level medical or technical function which is essential to the<br>participation of_Individual Neutral Athletes_may be granted<br>accreditation.<br>Notwithstanding the above eligibility, the provisions of the UCI<br>Regulations (cf. article 19 of the Ad hoc Rules) allowing an event<br>organiser to exclude the participation of a rider or a team remain<br>applicable.|
|---|---|
|**National teams**|In accordance with article 4 of the Ad hoc Rules, the participation<br>of national teams composed of riders of Russian or Belarusian<br>sporting nationality is not permitted in events on the UCI<br>International Calendar.|
|**Neutrality**|Any participation of a rider of Russian or Belarusian sporting<br>nationality and support personnel must fully comply with the UCI<br>Regulations, including the Anti-Doping rules, the Olympic Charter<br>and any other regulations applicable to events on the UCI<br>International Calendar concerning the conditions of participation.<br>In addition, the strictest neutrality towards the Russian<br>Federation and the Republic of Belarus at any time since the<br>beginning of the war in Ukraine is required for any participation in|

|**Flags**|Flags of the Russian Federation or the Republic of Belarus<br>(current or historical) may not be raised or displayed at any<br>competition venue, nor in any area where ancillary events are<br>held, or any other area controlled by the organiser of an event on<br>the UCI International Calendar. This requirement does not apply<br>to statutory meetings in which the National Federations of Russia<br>and/or Belarus are authorised to take part.<br>Where the use of a flag is necessary to identify_Individual Neutral_<br>_Athletes_(e.g., at ceremonies, protocol events, on-site, TV/media<br>displays), either an all-white flag shall be used or no flag shall be<br>used.|

|**Anthems**|The national anthems of Russia and Belarus (or any other<br>anthem associated with the Russian Federation or the Republic<br>of Belarus) shall not be played or sung at any time during an<br>event on the UCI International Calendar in any competition<br>venue, nor in any area where ancillary events are held, or any<br>other area controlled by the organiser of an event on the UCI<br>International Calendar.<br>The anthem to be used to represent any_Individual Neutral_<br>_Athlete_at an official ceremony (opening or closing, protocol,<br>medal ceremony, etc.) shall be Beethoven's Ode to Joy.|
|**Uniforms**|The uniform worn by_Individual Neutral Athletes_and their support<br>personnel shall be of a solid light blue colour.<br>The<br>uniforms<br>(whether<br>competition,<br>warm-up,<br>formal,<br>ceremonial, casual, etc.), clothing, equipment, accessories and<br>personal items belonging to_Individual Neutral Athletes_and their<br>support personnel that are worn, used and/or displayed at<br>competition venues, or any area where ancillary events are held,<br>or any other area controlled by the organiser, shall not contain:<br>-<br>Any recognition of, or reference to, the Russian<br>Federation, the Republic of Belarus, or their National<br>Federations or NOCs, or any national identification of<br>Russia or Belarus (such as flag, coat of arms or any other<br>national symbol or emblem, whether official or unofficial)<br>in any language or format;<br>-<br>Any emblem or identification, commercial or otherwise, of<br>any Russian or Belarusian organisation or entity;<br>-<br>Any expression (message, graphic element, symbol, etc)<br>with a direct or indirect political connotation.<br>Any clothing used by an_Individual Neutral Athlete_or his support<br>personnel must be submitted to the UCI for approval. Any<br>application must be submitted at least one month before the<br>event in question. Any use of clothing other than the one<br>approved by the UCI may result in the rider's refusal to start or<br>disqualification.|
|**Medals**|_Individual Neutral Athletes_may receive medals for their<br>performances and participate in the relevant award ceremonies.<br>Due to the participation as individuals, these medals shall not<br>count towards any medal table by nations.|

|**Commissaires**|International Commissaires who hold Russian or Belarusian<br>passports shall not display or express, by any means, an<br>association with their nationality.<br>International Commissaires must meet the conditions of<br>neutrality stipulated above in order to be appointed by the UCI to<br>officiate at an event on the UCI International Calendar.<br>Their involvement in events on the UCI International Calendar<br>shall not be associated in any way with the flags, names,<br>acronyms, emblems etc. of their country of nationality.|
