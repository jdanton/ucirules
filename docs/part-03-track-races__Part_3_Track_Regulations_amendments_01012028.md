• Madison: 8 teams Six-Day Races Six-Day races shall not award UCI points; however, the competition may include standalone events of Class 1 or Class 2 category. These events must have the minimum participation requirements as listed above for UCI points to be awarded (number of nations and minimum number of riders). Minimum prize money must be awarded as per UCI requirements, when applicable. 

_1) In team events (Madison excluded), if a team is composed of riders from different nations (mixed team), the nation of the majority of riders shall prevail. In team events (Madison excluded), where no majority is possible, the nation of the participating rider shall not count. In Madison, the nations of all the riders taking part in the event shall be counted._ 

_2) Events from the Elite World Championships programme, organised for Men Elite and Women Elite categories._ 

_3) Both categories must reach Class 1 requirements to maintain the event in class 1 the following year._
