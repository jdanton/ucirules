## **PART 9 WORLD CHAMPIONSHIPS** 

**Rules amendments applying on 01.10.2025** 

## **Chapter II PARTICIPATION IN THE UCI WORLD CHAMPIONSHIPS** 

**9.2.064** Events selected for the Para Cycling World Championships are the following: 

## **Road** 

Road race – all divisions; Individual Time Trial – all divisions; Mixed Team relay – division H. 

## **Track** 

Kilometre <del>/500 m</del> – divisions B & C; Individual pursuit – divisions <del>B & C;</del> Mixed Team sprint – division C; Sprint – division B & C; Scratch race – division C; Elimination – division C; <del>Para Omnium – division C;</del> Mixed Tandem Team Sprint – division B.
