## **Annex 1 of the Ad Hoc Rules** 

## **Version on 01.07.2026** 

## **Conditions of Participation for individual neutral riders of Russian sporting nationality and their support personnel.** 

|**Participation**|The participation of riders of Russian sporting nationality and<br>their support personnel in events on the UCI International<br>Calendar is only authorised in the circumstances provided for in<br>articles 4 to 9 of the Ad Hoc Rules and only in an individual and<br>neutral capacity, and in no case as representatives of the<br>Russian Federation, their National Federation or NOC.<br>A rider participating in events on the UCI International Calendar<br>under the previous paragraph is considered as an "_Individual_<br>_Neutral Athlete_".<br>With regard to the support personnel, only persons holding a<br>high-level medical or technical function which is essential to the<br>participation of_Individual Neutral Athletes_may be granted<br>accreditation.|
|---|---|
|**Neutrality**|Any participation of a rider of Russian sporting nationality and<br>support personnel must fully comply with the UCI Regulations,<br>including the Anti-Doping rules, the Olympic Charter and any<br>other regulations applicable to events on the UCI International<br>Calendar concerning the conditions of participation.<br>In addition, the strictest neutrality towards the Russian<br>Federation at any time since the beginning of the war in Ukraine<br>is required for any participation in events on the UCI International<br>Calendar, whether as an_Individual Neutral Athlete_or with a UCI<br>team or club, according to the following points:<br>1)No link with the Russian military or with any other national<br>security agency<br>Riders and support personnel who are or have been contracted<br>to the Russian military, including any affiliated entities, or with<br>national security agencies cannot participate in events on the UCI<br>International Calendar.<br>2)No communication associated with Russia<br>Riders and support personnel must refrain from any activity or<br>communication, either verbal, non-verbal or written, associated<br>with the national flag, anthem, emblem or any other symbol of the<br>Russian Federation, its National Federation or NOC, or from any<br>support for the war in Ukraine in any official venue or in the media<br>(including interviews, social media, retweets and reposted<br>messages on X, forwarded messages, etc. ) at any time since the|

|**Anti-Doping**<br>**requirements**|All Riders of Russian sporting nationality must comply with all<br>anti-doping requirements applicable to them, in particular those<br>set out in the UCI Anti-Doping Rules.<br>With regard to_Individual Neutral Athletes_, the UCI shall<br>determine the specific procedures and rules to be fulfilled for their<br>participation in doping controls, in collaboration with its delegated<br>body for conducting doping controls in cycling, the International<br>Testing Agency (ITA).|
|**Application for status**<br>**of****_Individual Neutral_**<br>**_Athlete_**|For any participation in the Elite and Under 23 categories, riders<br>shall apply to the UCI to be granted the status of_Individual_<br>_Neutral Athlete_. Riders shall submit the relevant application form<br>(provided by the UCI) toneutralathletes@uci.ch.<br>Upon receipt of the application form, the UCI shall carry out<br>verifications to<br>ensure<br>compliance<br>with the<br>applicable<br>requirements for such status of_Individual Neutral Athlete_, in<br>particular those related to neutrality as set out above.<br>The UCI may apply a fee for the processing of the application in<br>case exceptional measures for the verification of the applicable<br>rules are required. In such a case, the processing of the<br>application shall be suspended until payment of the application<br>fee.<br>The UCI shall publish the list of riders to whom the status of<br>_Individual Neutral Athlete_has been granted.|

|**Flags**|Flags of the Russian Federation (current or historical) may not be<br>raised or displayed at any competition venue, nor in any area<br>where ancillary events are held, or any other area controlled by<br>the organiser of an event on the UCI International Calendar. This<br>requirement does not apply to statutory meetings in which the<br>National Federations of Russia is authorised to take part.<br>Where the use of a flag is necessary to identify_Individual Neutral_<br>_Athletes_(e.g., at ceremonies, protocol events, on-site, TV/media<br>displays), either an all-white flag shall be used or no flag shall be<br>used.<br>Each organiser of an event on the UCI International Calendar<br>shall be responsible for ensuring that no flag of the Russian<br>Federation, or any other object referring to this country, is<br>displayed within the scope of, or in relation to, an event, including<br>by spectators.|
|**Anthems**|The national anthem of Russia (or any other anthem associated<br>with the Russian Federation) shall not be played or sung at any<br>time during an event on the UCI International Calendar in any<br>competition venue, nor in any area where ancillary events are<br>held, or any other area controlled by the organiser of an event on<br>the UCI International Calendar.<br>The anthem to be used to represent any_Individual Neutral_<br>_Athlete_at an official ceremony (opening or closing, protocol,<br>medal ceremony, etc.) shall be Beethoven's Ode to Joy.|
