## MEMORANDUM 

## **PART 11 OLYMPIC GAMES Rules amendments applying on 01.03.2026** 

## **Chapter I GENERAL REGULATIONS** 

## **Equipment** 

**11.1.009** For track races, equipment identified in the UCI Track Equipment Registration Procedure <del>ss</del> which teams or riders intend to use during the Olympic Games must comply with the UCI regulations, and particularly with <del>Aa</del> rticle 1.3.006. 

The UCI Track Equipment Registration Procedure shall set out additional requirements applicable for use of equipment in Track cycling at the Olympic Games. 

<del>In addition, the equipment must have been previously used in one of the events listed in</del> For identified types of equipment, the UCI Track Equipment Registration Procedure <del>ss</del> shall set out requirements for the equipment to be registered prior to, and inspected and used at one of the listed events taking place the year preceding the Olympic Games. The relevant equipment shall <del>must</del> comply with requirements of commercial availability <del>at the time of use in one of the listed events</del> as of the date of the event selected by the National Federation for inspection and use of the equipment and <del>shall continuously</del> comply with such requirements continuously until the Olympic Games. 

<del>The UCI shall register any equipment which teams or riders intend to use during track races at the Olympic Games in accordance with the UCI Track Equipment Registration Process and reserves the right to communicate about equipment which may be used and/or which may not be used.</del> 

The UCI Track Equipment Registration Procedure shall set out specific and additional requirements related to commercial availability of identified types of equipment, including maximum prices, as well as limitations to the number of items or models of equipment that may be registered and/or selected by National Federations. 

In addition, <del>Aa</del> ll remaining equipment which teams or riders intend to use at the Olympic Games must have been commercially available – in accordance with article 1.3.006 – as from its time of use in a prior competition - if applicable - and no later than 120 days prior to the Olympic Games, and shall comply with such requirements continuously until the Olympic Games. <del>at the latest a day after the UCI Track World Championships of the year preceding of the Olympic Games.</del>
