PART II - ROAD RACES

Rules amendments applying on 19.10.2026

Chapter II GENERAL PROVISIONS

(numbering of the articles modified on 01.01.05).

§ 1 Participation

  • 2.2.003 (…)
Special provisions for Men Under 23

Men Under 23 riders registered with a UCI WorldTeam or a UCI ProTeam are not authorised to participate in 1.2U and 2.2U class events, nor in the national championships held for the Under 23 category. Such restriction does not apply to Men Under 23 riders registered as trainees with UCI WorldTeams or UCI ProTeams according to articles 2.15.110bis or 2.16.033.

(text modified on 01.01.05; 01.01.07; 26.01.08; 01.02.12; 01.07.12; 01.10.13; 01.01.15; 01.01.18; 23.10.19; 01.01.20; 12.06.20; 01.11.22; 01.11.25; 19.10.26).

Chapter X UCI RANKINGS

§ 1 Elite and Under 23 Men’s UCI World Ranking
Special Provisions for Riders Competing in Other Cycling Disciplines

2.10.004 As from season 2027, points earned at the UCI World Championships in Track, Quarter Mountain Bike XCO, Cyclo-Cross, and Gravel, as well as in the final standings of the UCI World Cup or UCI Nations Cup in Track, Mountain Bike XCO, and Cyclo-Cross, may be added to the UCI Road Team Ranking based on a specific points scale.

These additional points are included in the team ranking only if the rider is among the top 20 riders within his team’s Men Elite ranking. No points from these disciplines are added to the individual UCI Road Rankings.

(article introduced on 19.10.26).

MEMORANDUM 29 September 2026

Chapter XVII UCI CONTINENTAL TEAMS

§ 1 General Conditions

2.17.004 A UCI Continental team or UCI women’s continental team will comprise riders who may or may not be professional, in the elite and/or under 23 categories. It must have minimum 10 riders for UCI continental teams, 8 for UCI women’s continental teams and a maximum of 16 riders for both categories.

However, a UCI continental team or a UCI women’s continental team shall also have the right to add up to 4 riders specialising in the following cycling disciplines: cyclo-cross; mountain bike: cross-country; track: endurance, madison. This shall be subject to the riders in question being ranked among the top 150 of the last final UCI individual classification for cyclo-cross and mountain bike cross-country and, for track, being ranked among the top 150 in the endurance or madison ranking at any time over the course of the last 52 weeks preceding the rider’s date of registration with the team, or having competed in the UCI Track World Championships or Continental Championships of the current or previous (track) season.

(text modified on 01.07.09; 01.07.12; 01.01.17; 01.07.18; 01.01.20; 01.11.25; 06.02.26)

2.17.004 All UCI Continental Team and UCI Women’s Continental Team Sports bis directors and assistant sports directors must hold the UCI Sports Director Diploma in accordance with the provisions of article 1.1.077.

(Article introduced on 19.10.26).
