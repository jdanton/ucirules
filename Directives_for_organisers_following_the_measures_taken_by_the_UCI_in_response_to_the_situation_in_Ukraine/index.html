Directives for organisers following the measures taken by the UCI in response to the situation in Ukraine

Updated version – in force as of 1[st] July 2026

Following the amendments to the UCI Ad Hoc Rules adopted by the UCI Management Committee on 2 June 2026, entering into force on 1[st] July 2026, the following directives must be applied by organisers and timing companies:

Russia:

The following restrictions on the participation of riders of Russian sporting nationality have been amended:

  • Riders can now compete together in team events where the competition format requires collective participation (such as the Madison, Team Sprint, Team Pursuit Team Relay, Team Time Trial, and Mixed Team Relay and any other team event according to the UCI Regulations). This applies to all events of the UCI International Calendar.

  • Riders may take part in any Junior events[1] on the UCI International Calendar, including UCI World Cup, UCI World Championships and UEC European Championships without being required to obtain Individual Neutral Athlete status beforehand. The same applies to Support Personnel of riders. Their participation in said events shall remain as neutral athletes, meaning that all national emblems, flags and anthems remain forbidden.

The restrictions on distinctive signs below are maintained for all cycling categories and disciplines on the UCI International Calendar. They apply to all official documents related to the event (including, but not limited to, start lists, results, rankings, TV graphics, official documents, etc.):

  • The appearance of all emblems, names, acronyms, colours, flags and anthems linked to Russia is prohibited;

  • The flag of Russia must be removed or replaced by a white flag;

  • The name of the country, as well as the acronym of Russia (RUS), must be deleted and left blank;

  • Where the use of a flag is necessary to identify riders of Russian sporting nationality (e.g., at ceremonies, protocol events, on-site, TV/media displays), either an all-white flag shall be used or no flag shall be used.

1 N.B. The rules for participation in other categories remain unchanged, meaning that a Junior rider is required to obtain Individual Neutral Athlete status as a prior condition for registration in any U23 or Elite UCI World Cup, UCI World Championships or UEC European Championships. The same applies to Support Personnel. Exceptions to the requirement of holding Individual Neutral Athlete status, as provided under article 5 and 8 of the UCI Ad Hoc Rules also continue to apply.

  • The anthem to be used to represent any rider of Russian sporting nationality at an official ceremony (opening or closing, protocol, medal ceremony, etc.) shall be Beethoven's Ode to Joy.

  • When uploading results to the UCI DataRide platform, organisers (or timing companies) must use the usual code (RUS) to ensure that points and rankings are calculated correctly. The UCI shall ensure that the display of results and rankings on the UCI website does not show the flag and acronym of Russia;

If you have any questions about UCI DataRide, please contact our support team at ucidataridehelp@uci.ch. Our team will answer your questions as soon as possible.

Belarus:

The restrictions put in place in 2022 have been lifted. The participation of Belarusian riders, on an individual basis, with teams or national teams is governed by the UCI Regulations, without any specific requirements or restrictions. Likewise, all restrictions relating to protocol matters in respect of emblems, names, acronyms, flags and anthems are lifted.
