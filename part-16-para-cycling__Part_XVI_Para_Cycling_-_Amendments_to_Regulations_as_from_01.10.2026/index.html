PART XVI - PARA CYCLING

Rules Amendments applying on 01.10.2026

  • 16.8.009 (N) The event shall at least include:

  • a 200 meters time trial, flying start;

The number of semi finals and the compoisition of semi finals and finals is based on entered athletes in the event.

The distance to accomplish including the momentum and the 200 metres is fixed as follows, depending on the length of the track:

  • 250 metres track or smaller: 3.5 laps

  • 285,714 metres track: 3.0 laps

  • 333,33 metres track: 2.5 laps

  • 400 metres track and longer: 2.0 laps.
