The bids for allocation of a UCI World Championship shall be examined by the Executive Committee, which will make an assessment of relevant criteria and submit its recommendation preselection of the candidates that it deems the most appropriate for guaranteeing a high level organisation and will submit these candidates to the UCI Management Committee. Only bids which meet the requirements set out in the bidding guide and for which a binding organisation agreement has been signed shall be taken into consideration by the Executive Committee

The UCI M anagement Committee will seek a consensus when allocating a UCI World Championships.

Failing a consensus, a vote will be held. Members of the nationality of the organiser shall not take part in the vote. A secret ballot will be held upon request of any member.

In the event of a consensus to reject a candidate, such candidate shall not take part in the vote.

If more than two candidates remain, a further vote will be held among the candidates which have obtained the largest number of votes in the first round. If two candidates are tied for the second place in the first round, a further vote will be held among the candidates placed first and second, until only two candidates remain.

The organisation of the UCI World Championships will be allocated on condition that full agreement exists concerning the organisation contract, or any other condition specified by the UCI Management Committee.
