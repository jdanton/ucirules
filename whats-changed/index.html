What changed

A plain-English summary of the substantive rule changes pulled in the 2026-06-08 sync of the UCI Cycling Regulations. Article numbers are cited where the amendment documents give them; open the corresponding regulation in the sidebar for the full text.

Unofficial summary. The PDFs on uci.org are authoritative.

Part 1 — General Organisation

In force 01.07.2026

  • Art. 1.2.029 — National road championship results must be submitted via UCI DataRide within two days of the event; late results are excluded from the UCI classification.
  • Art. 1.3.10bis — Shared bicycle technical specifications now apply to road, track and cyclo-cross competitions (the term "racing" was removed).

Rule changes for 01.01.2028

  • New Art. 1.3.006bis (Onboard technology) — Comprehensive new rule on data-capturing/transmitting devices: positioning, image, mechanical (power, speed, cadence, tyre pressure…) and limited physiological data (heart rate, body temperature — viewable only by the rider) are allowed; in-competition capture of metabolic data such as glucose or lactate is prohibited. Image devices must generally be bike-mounted (not worn) and need organiser approval. Head units capped at 126 × 71 mm, only four mount types permitted.
  • Art. 1.3.018 (Wheels & tyres) — New wheel "Approval Protocol": all road/track/cyclo-cross wheels need UCI approval unless they meet "traditional wheel" criteria. Mass-start rims capped at 65 mm height (≥12 spokes); road/track inflated wheel diameter max 700 mm; cyclo-cross tyre width max 33 mm (no spikes/studs); wheels must pass ISO 4210-2:2023 impact testing.

Part 2 — Road Races

Amendments 01.07.2026

  • Art. 2.1.005bis / 2.1.007 — Grand Tour wild cards restricted to the 30 best-ranked teams; for 2026–2028 wild cards allocated so 23 teams start each Grand Tour. ProTeams taking an obligatory Grand Tour invitation pay the WorldTeam biological-passport contribution.
  • Art. 2.2.017 — Codifies a protected finish zone (≥300 m before / 100 m after the line); continuous weighted barriers required, lightweight/plastic barriers banned.
  • Art. 2.2.024 + new 2.2.025bis — Maximum size for rider race-radio units (150 × 60 × 35 mm); jersey pockets must be on the back, with a front pocket allowed only to hold the radio.
  • Art. 2.2.040 — Driver/passenger exclusions changed to an automatic suspension until the next event in the series (or 7 days, whichever is shorter).
  • Art. 2.6.007 / 2.6.034 — Junior world-circuit stage races extended from a maximum of 4 to 5 days.
  • Modernisation throughout — all "fax" references replaced with email/internet.

Amendments 01.09.2026

  • New Chapter XIV §1 (Arts. 2.14.001–2.14.019) — Creates a single combined UCI Junior Road Nations Cup (men + women), replacing the separate men's and women's competitions (former §3/§4 abrogated). Each event must include both a Men Junior and Women Junior race.
  • Art. 2.14.007 — Junior Road Nations Cup teams reduced to a maximum of 5 riders (minimum 4 to start).
  • Art. 2.14.016 — Grand Final is a stage race of at least 5 days, with continental qualification quotas.

Amendments 01.01.2027

  • Art. 2.2.015 — Event safety manager must hold a (non-expiring) UCI certification, be identifiable by uniform/badge, be named in the technical guide, and attend the UCI's annual meeting. Consolidates course-marking and unpaved-road conditions.
  • New Art. 2.12.003bis (Yellow cards) — Yellow cards extended to WorldTour, Women's WorldTour, ProSeries, Class 1, Olympics, Worlds and Continental Championships; CHF 2,000 fine if the team fails to identify the responsible person.

Part 3 — Track Races

Amendments 01.07.2026

  • Art. 3.10.008 — Race-incident penalty table restructured into three columns by event level; new fines for non-compliant bicycle accessories (2.6.1) and rider-worn accessories (3.2.1): CHF 200/100/50 plus ranking-point loss or one-place relegation. No world record recognised if a non-compliant accessory was used.

Amendments 01.09.2026

  • Art. 3.2.001 — Only riders aged 17+ may race internationally; 2nd-year juniors may enter U23/elite events.
  • World/Hour records (Art. 3.5.005 ff.) — "Special attempt" records eliminated, equipment must be submitted 30 days (was 15) ahead, and a new Hour-record mishap-restart procedure introduced.
  • Art. 3.4.007 — World Cup national-team entry capped at 1 team (was 2) in Team Sprint and Team Pursuit.
  • Sprint rules centralised (new 3.2.017); Team Pursuit incomplete teams now recorded DNS rather than disqualified.

Rule changes for 01.01.2028

  • Art. 3.8.003 — Updated Class 1/Class 2 calendar criteria: minimum events, minimum nations (4 / 3) and minimum riders per event.

Part 5 — Cyclo-cross

The source labels these "Part IV Cyclo-cross", but they are Cyclo-cross changes.

Rule changes 2026 (in force 01.07.2026)

  • Art. 5.1.008bis — First-aid requirements rewritten: drops the ambulance/doctor mandate in favour of ≥1 first-aid post and ≥4 first-aid qualified people per competition; medical coverage now mandatory at all official training sessions.
  • Art. 5.1.024 — Plank section may be removed if the course is abnormally slippery; planks may be replaced by a tree log ≤40 cm thick.
  • Art. 5.2.003 — Single one-year rolling ranking for all categories, with best-result tie-breaking.

Rule changes 2027

  • Arts. 5.1.001 / 5.1.004 / 5.1.048 — Women Junior become a standalone category: they no longer race with Women Elite, Class 1/2 organisers must run a Women Junior race, and new race durations are set (Women Junior 40 min, U23 45, Elite 50).
  • Arts. 5.2.001 ff. — Women Junior get their own ranking; junior best-results point-counting limits now apply to both men's and women's juniors.

Part 8 — Indoor Cycling

Amendments 01.07.2026

  • Art. 8.7.043 — Cycle-ball U23 Elite-League age limit lowered to 22 (from 23).
  • Art. 8.11.001 — Cycle-ball World Championships Elite-league schedule restructured (24 games instead of 25; revised bracket and placement matches).

Amendments 01.01.2027

  • Cycle-ball play (Arts. 8.8.003 / 8.8.005 / 8.8.053) — Adds an explicit three-part test for illegal "blocking" (proximity, direction, movement), clarifies boarding play, and refines penalty-kick offences.
  • Artistic cycling (Arts. 8.3.011 ff.) — Large revision of Pair and Team-4 figure definitions and point-value tables (technical clarifications).

Anti-doping, discipline & testing

Anti-Doping Tribunal — amendments 01.07.2026

  • Single Judges now appointed by the Tribunal President, not the Secretariat (Art. 11.1.f); the UCI is represented by the ITA and/or external counsel, replacing UCI Legal Anti-Doping Services (Art. 12.3).

Part 12 Discipline & Procedures — amendments 01.07.2026

  • Road Sub-Committee jurisdiction now turns only on severity (≤30 days and/or ≤CHF 5,000), not event tier (Art. 12.5.004); fee for a reasoned decision cut from CHF 3,000 to 1,000 (Art. 12.6.027).

Testing & Investigations (TIR) — amendments 05.06.2026

  • Biological-passport blood sampling: post-activity waiting period cut from 2 hours to 60 minutes (rider chaperoned); altitude-disclosure look-back extended from 2 to 4 weeks; new form questions on prior stage-racing and upcoming competition. Also republished in full as the new UCI Testing and Investigations Regulations (05.06.2026).

Ad Hoc Rules — Russia / Belarus measures (in force 01.07.2026)

  • Belarus removed from scope — participation bans, sponsorship limits and distinctive-sign rules now apply only to Russia; Belarusian riders, teams, events and brands are no longer covered (§4–30). Rules re-approved 2 June 2026.
  • Sponsorship (§23–24) — Russian brand visibility may be authorised if the applicant proves no link to the Russian Federation or war effort (presumed met if the sponsor has publicly denounced the war).
  • Annex 1 (Individual Neutral Athletes) — Belarus removed; the IOC-recommendation preamble deleted; the 30-day deadline for the UCI to confirm neutral status removed (and a processing fee may be charged); competition uniform must be a solid light-blue colour pre-approved by the UCI.
  • Art. 2.2.001 (Road teams' participation, 05.06.2026) — Reference table of which teams may not race the same event; teams marked * use the Art. 2.1.005bis participation rules. (Team listings are embedded as images in the source PDF.)
